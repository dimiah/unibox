Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Unified Inbox Search ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool built and operated by its sole developer for their own use. This policy explains how the app handles data from the Gmail accounts it is connected to.

Who runs this app

The app is run entirely on the developer's own computer. It is not a hosted service, is not offered to other users, and no one other than the developer has access to it or to any data it stores.

What data it accesses

When a Gmail account is authorized, the app requests access via Google's OAuth consent flow to:

  • Read, search, and display the content of email messages in that account (subject, sender, recipients, body text, attachments, and labels).
  • Mark messages as read or unread, archive messages, and add or remove labels.
  • Send email replies from that account, when the user chooses to send one.

Where data is stored

Recent email content is copied into a local SQLite database stored only on the developer's own computer, so it can be searched quickly without repeated calls to Gmail. Older, untagged messages are automatically deleted from this local copy after a short retention window; the original messages are never deleted from Gmail by this process. Attachments are only downloaded on request and cached locally.

No data the app accesses is transmitted to any third party, any server outside the developer's own computer, or any analytics, advertising, or AI service. The app does not use the Gmail data it accesses to train any machine learning or AI model.

Data sharing

This app does not share, sell, or transfer any Gmail data to anyone. There are no other users, integrations, or external services involved.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from a Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access stops the app from reading or modifying that account going forward; it does not affect any email already present in the Gmail account itself.

Changes to this policy

Since this is a personal tool, this policy may be updated as the app's functionality changes. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how the app handles data can be directed to its developer, Dilu, at dm95ba@gmail.com.
